Back when I was working remotely during the pandemic, I found myself wanting a personal publishing platform of my own—somewhere beyond social media where I could store my thoughts for the long term.

I did consider setting up a blog using WordPress, but it required spending a small amount of money on server hosting and a domain. Hesitating on even that minor cost, I let time slip by without taking action.

I recently discovered a perfect web-hosting service called **GitHub Pages**. After checking its features, workflow, and setup steps with ChatGPT, I realized I could build everything **completely for free** and decided to give it a try.

### GitHub Pages in the Age of Generative AI

Because you create the website files yourself and publish them to a GitHub repository, it might feel a bit intimidating for those without a systems engineering background. 

After creating my GitHub account, I had Claude Code guide me through the steps one by one, which finally led to the publication of this very first post.

I highly recommend this as an option for upskilling, especially for desk-bound professionals:

- Thanks to tools like **Claude Code**, we’ve entered an era where you can achieve high-precision application development simply by giving prompt instructions—even if you aren't a programmer (often called "**Vibe Coding**"). By mastering just the basics of **Git & GitHub**, you gain the ability to develop and manage your own applications independently.

- I write my blog posts in **Markdown format** (files with a `.md` extension). Because it’s a structured text file, it’s the optimal format for getting generative AI to accurately read information. Anyone who uses AI in their daily work has nothing to lose by learning how to write in Markdown.

- There are various development tools out there, but in my case, I use **Visual Studio** for everything from website design to blog creation. Its preview function—which shows how pages written in Markdown will actually look on a website—has been an absolute lifesaver.

Unlike typical blog platforms, setting this up involved using a variety of tools and techniques to publish the site. That multifaceted learning process is precisely why choosing **GitHub Pages** felt like a great way to acquire and maintain IT skills suited for the Generative AI era.

Moving forward, I hope to use this space to record various tips I pick up along the way, the growth of the plants in my garden, and all kinds of little milestones in everyday life.
