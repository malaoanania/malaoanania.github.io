In early November last year (2025), I happened to find a Koa tree online and bought it.

I knew that Koa is a tree native to Hawaii and used as a material for ukuleles and surfboards, but I never expected to find one in Japan, so I couldn't resist buying it.

One of the main reasons I wanted to start this blog in the first place was to keep a growth journal for my Koa tree. However, before I finally got around to launching the blog, the tree grew much faster and bigger than I ever anticipated.

**The Koa Tree Back in November 2025**
![Koa1](/assets/img/20261011_koa1.jpeg)

The photo above shows the Koa tree right after it arrived at my house.

It’s a bit hard to tell because the photo was taken from above, but its height was about 15 cm above the soil, and it was planted in a No. 4 pot (approx. 12 cm in diameter).

The main stem (if it could even be called a "trunk" at that stage) was less than 5 mm thick, so I remember handling it extremely carefully, fearing it might snap at any moment.

Since it seemed very unlikely to survive the winter in Yokohama, I kept a close eye on it indoors for six months until Golden Week the following year.

### Outdoor Debut ###

In early May 2026, I repotted it into a larger container and moved it outdoors.

Here is a quick chronological summary of its growth from that point up to today.

| Date | Growth Stage | Observation Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Around June 2026 | Flowering & Fruiting Observations | The tree bloomed. Started observing how the flowers opened and how seed pods formed afterward. |
| Late July 2026 | Elongation of Seed Pods | Confirmed long green seed pods growing. This was the stage where fruits developed after flowering. |
| August 4, 2026 | Further Pod Growth | Pods grew to about 10–15 cm, with seeds starting to swell inside. |
| September 5, 2026 | Maturation of Pods | Observed multiple pods turning from green to yellowish-green, with some shifting to reddish-brown. Noted that each pod matures at a different pace. |
| September 15, 2026 | Considering Harvest Time | Dark brown pods appeared, indicating that the seed harvesting season was approaching. |
| September 27, 2026 | Considering First Trial Harvest | The number of brown pods increased; checked if they were ready for harvesting. |
| October 1, 2026 | First Seed Harvest | Harvested one brown pod and extracted several seeds from inside. Successfully harvested seeds from a Koa tree grown right here in Yokohama! |
| From October 2026 | Seed Storage & Germination Prep | Stored the harvested seeds and started preparing for scarification/germination and sowing. Helping the parent tree survive the coming winter is also the next challenge. |

### The Koa Tree Today ###

In less than a year since last November, the tree has grown to nearly 180 cm tall.

Thanks in part to advice from ChatGPT, I even managed to harvest seeds recently.

My next challenge is to try sprouting the seeds into seedlings, while keeping a watchful eye on the parent tree to see if it can survive this winter outdoors.
